    Manual for Shift Manager¶

    Welcome as a Shift Manager for the Eurofurence Critter System. Your main task is to create shifts for the events that your team manages. Critters are any volunteers who would like to offer their help during the convention. They can sign up for available shifts through the system. While use of the system is mandatory for critters, it is encouraged that departments also use this system to organize their shifts with their team members.

    Login¶

    The first step is to log in to the Critter system. To do this, open the main page https://critter.eurofurence.org and select the Login with Eurofurence Identity button. You use the username and password of your regular Eurofurence account, which you also use to log in to other Eurofurence sites. This is the only way to log into the system.

    Registration¶

    There is no registration required, just login using the IDP. Once you login with your account you will be redirected to the user profile page where you need to fill in any missing information. There you enter your Telegram name, select the crittertypes you want to join and decide whether you want to receive email notifications.

    Attention

    It may happen that no critter types are displayed for joining. You can still complete the registration in this case. In this case, please contact a Critter System Admin afterwards.
